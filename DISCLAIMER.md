## Appropriate use

Use Nuclear-grade to improve:

- design basis clarity;
- configuration discipline;
- traceability from claims to evidence;
- verification and release readiness;
- dependency/model/API trust decisions;
- AI-assisted development controls;
- incident learning and corrective action;
- proportional rigor by consequence.

Do not use Nuclear-grade alone to justify regulated operation, safety significance, procurement acceptance, licensing, certification, or formal quality-program adequacy.

Do not describe a Nuclear-grade packet as formal V&V, NQA-1 evidence, regulator-facing evidence, safety-basis evidence, procurement evidence, or a quality-assurance record unless an authorized organization has separately adopted, reviewed, and approved that use under its governing requirements.
