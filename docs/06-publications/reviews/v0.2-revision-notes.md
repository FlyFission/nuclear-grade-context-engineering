# Academic Preprint v0.2 — Revision Notes

## Manuscript

**Title:** *From Context to Accountable Acceptance: A Graded, Git-Native Control Method for AI-Assisted Software Changes*

**Publication posture:** discussion preprint; not submitted or peer reviewed.

## Changes made from v0.1

1. Rewrote the abstract and contribution hierarchy around **evidence self-authorship**.
2. Added an explicit falsifiable prediction and corresponding preregistered study design.
3. Replaced the five-rung independence ladder with a five-axis **actor–evidence independence profile** and named evidence patterns.
4. Treated the profile as a partial order; human witnessing is no longer implied to dominate diverse mechanical verification.
5. Recast the acceptance tuple as an **acceptance-record schema** with operational acceptance and revalidation predicates.
6. Added a threat model separating epistemic, security, configuration, provenance, operational-authorization, and human-review failures.
7. Added a four-level Git/evidence trust model and clarified that Git-native does not imply immutability, authentication, append-only storage, or independence.
8. Added and differentiated the closest verified adjacent work:
   - Software Delegation Contracts;
   - Governed AI-Assisted Engineering;
   - ContextNest;
   - Runtime Governance for AI Agents;
   - From Agent Traces to Trust;
   - in-toto and SLSA.
9. Removed the three-decimal author-scored results table and renamed the section **Formative Design Inspection**.
10. Retained raw scores in the repository for auditability while reporting only qualitative patterns in the paper.
11. Reduced academic-method density by demoting lifecycle mnemonics, packet filenames, and context-pack detail to implementation vehicles.
12. Consolidated repeated boundary language and rewrote the conclusion around the central prediction rather than the lifecycle.
13. Kept the neutral academic title; Nuclear-grade Context Engineering remains the project/reference-implementation name.
