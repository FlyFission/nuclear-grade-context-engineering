## Controlling decision

RC1 remains a viable `cs.SE` discussion preprint candidate, but the exact PDF should not advance unchanged. RC2 will close two release blockers and a bounded set of precision, reproducibility, and study-design findings. It will not add a universal coupling score, policy engine, efficacy claim, broad platform implementation, or new empirical result.

## Accepted release blockers

1. Replace the unsupported “consequence-graded admissibility rule” claim with a consistent proposal/principle and an external versioned-policy boundary.
2. Repair the overlapping labels in Figure 1's separated-evidence path.

## Qualified or rejected recommendations

- **Universal consequence-to-profile table:** qualified. RC2 will not invent one normative mapping. It will specify an external policy function plus an illustrative case.
- **Full independent efficacy study before discussion-preprint circulation:** rejected as a current gate. The absence remains explicit and blocks future efficacy claims.
- **Rename the method or remove FlyFission affiliation:** rejected as a blocker; the paper title already leads with the scientific problem and the method boundary remains explicit.
- **Repository implementation or trial artifacts may be missing:** rejected after clean execution and inspection of assessed commit `92357cd`.
- **All future-dated references are suspect:** rejected. Primary arXiv checks validated the two reviewed load-bearing records. Archived first-party OpenAI pages were subsequently recovered through the Internet Archive and support the bounded SWE-bench claims; live-page access remains a submission-time check.
