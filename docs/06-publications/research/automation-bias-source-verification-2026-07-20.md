# Automation-use and automation-bias source verification

**Date checked:** 2026-07-20
**Purpose:** Verify two reviewer-suggested human-factors sources before adding them to the RC2 study-design framing.

## Parasuraman and Riley (1997)

- **Title:** *Humans and Automation: Use, Misuse, Disuse, Abuse*
- **Authors:** Raja Parasuraman and Victor Riley
- **Venue:** *Human Factors*, 39(2), 230–253
- **DOI:** `10.1518/001872097778543886`
- **Primary metadata source:** Crossref DOI record
- **Verified use:** Supports distinguishing appropriate automation use from misuse, disuse, and abuse. It is background for reviewer reliance, not evidence that the paper's method improves decisions.

## Skitka, Mosier, and Burdick (1999)

- **Title:** *Does Automation Bias Decision-Making?*
- **Authors:** Linda J. Skitka, Kathleen L. Mosier, and Mark Burdick
- **Venue:** *International Journal of Human-Computer Studies*, 51(5), 991–1006
- **DOI:** `10.1006/ijhc.1999.0252`
- **Primary metadata source:** Crossref DOI record
- **Verified use:** Supports automation bias as established human-factors lineage relevant to reviewer instructions and reliance measures. It does not validate actor–evidence coupling or the proposed study condition.

## Decision

Both sources are admitted for bounded related-work and study-design context. Neither is presented as evidence of the repository method's efficacy, novelty, or effect size.
