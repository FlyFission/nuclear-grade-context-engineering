# Academic Preprint v0.3 — Novelty Review Decision Log

## Trigger

Ben requested an exhaustive novelty and competitiveness review, with specific concern about Palantir Ontology and whether the manuscript was differentiated enough to compete.

## Decision

Proceed with a narrower v0.3 rather than withdrawing the paper.

The review found publishable residual novelty in **evidence custody and actor–evidence coupling**, but substantial prior art for the broader lifecycle, evidence bundles, artifact gates, professional self-review, assurance cases, runtime governance, proposal/approval workflows, operational ontologies, and verdict/apply separation.

## Accepted changes

1. Retitle the manuscript around the narrow scientific object: actor–evidence coupling.
2. Treat evidence self-authorship as an agent-specific form of established self-review, not a first discovery.
3. Add empirical lineage from LLM self-preference and preference-leakage research.
4. Add the closest new software-review and evidence-artifact work:
   - *Human Oversight for AI-Generated Test Artifacts*;
   - *Artifact Gate Evaluation for AI-Assisted Software Delivery*;
   - *From Traceability to Reviewability*.
5. Add AgentBound as adjacent runtime-governance work.
6. Add Palantir Ontology, proposal review, and scenario application as industrial prior art.
7. Demote verdict/apply-clearance from a supporting contribution to an established-adjacent implementation primitive.
8. Define **evidence custody** as control over generation, selection, transformation, execution environment, retention, and presentation.
9. Recast the empirical agenda to isolate evidence separation from the already-studied benefits of contracts and artifact bundles.

## Claims rejected after review

The paper does not claim novelty for:

- professional self-review concerns;
- artifact-aware delivery or artifact gates;
- evidence bundles or acceptance records;
- intent–claim–evidence review structures;
- operational ontologies;
- proposal/review/merge workflows;
- scenario versus live application;
- runtime authorization;
- provenance, signed attestations, or trace reconstruction;
- consequence-graded oversight; or
- verdict/apply-clearance as a general concept.

## Defensible residual claim

> Provenance and gate protection are insufficient when the change actor controls the evidence path. Acceptance should therefore model actor–evidence coupling and impose consequence-dependent independence constraints on decisive evidence.

## Publication posture

- Credible discussion preprint or position paper: yes.
- Strong empirical-software-engineering main-track paper: not yet.
- Open artifact contribution: potentially credible.
- Improved safety, correctness, reviewer performance, defect reduction, or organizational outcomes: not demonstrated.
