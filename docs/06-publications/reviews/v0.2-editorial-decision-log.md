# Academic Preprint v0.2 — Editorial Decision Log

Date: 2026-07-19
Manuscript: *From Context to Accountable Acceptance*
Input: five external review sets supplied by Ben Huffer

## Editorial decision

Revise before public preprint submission. The reviews converge on a publishable discussion paper with a narrow conceptual contribution, a credible implementation artifact, and insufficient evidence for efficacy claims. The revision will strengthen the conceptual paper rather than add more method.

## Accepted changes

1. **Center evidence self-authorship.** Use the specific term consistently. Open the abstract and introduction with the coupled evidence-path failure mode.
2. **Narrow the contribution hierarchy.** Treat evidence self-authorship and actor–evidence independence as primary; verdict/apply-clearance and the reference implementation as supporting. Treat lifecycle, packet names, and mnemonics as implementation detail.
3. **Replace the ordinal independence ladder.** The five axes are orthogonal and do not form a total order. Replace “rungs” with an independence profile plus named evidence patterns. Do not imply that human witnessing always dominates diverse mechanical verification.
4. **Demote the evaluation to formative design inspection.** Remove the table of three-decimal author-scored means. Report qualitative patterns and retain raw scores only in the repository.
5. **Add an explicit falsification condition.** The central prediction fails if controlled studies show no meaningful reviewer-detection benefit from evidence separation, or if gate protection alone bounds acceptance error equally well.
6. **Add a threat model.** Separate epistemic error and persuasive omission from malicious actors, authorization failure, compromised toolchains, stale baselines, and timing/operational-state failures. State which controls address which threats.
7. **Add a Git/evidence trust model.** Ordinary Git records are reviewable but weakly authenticated. Distinguish ordinary Git, protected Git plus CI, signed attestations, and externally retained or append-only evidence. Git-native is an integration choice, not a trust guarantee.
8. **Simplify the academic taxonomy.** Keep three load-bearing concepts: the acceptance record, evidence self-authorship/independence, and consequence-graded rigor. Compress lifecycle and packet details.
9. **Treat the acceptance tuple as a schema.** Remove equation-style presentation unless it constrains acceptance. Add an operational acceptance predicate and revalidation condition in prose.
10. **Consolidate disclaimers.** Keep one concise boundary in the introduction and one method boundary. Preserve local evidence limitations where directly relevant; remove repetitive general caveats.
11. **Neutral scientific object, branded implementation.** Keep the neutral paper title. Call the scientific object “graded accountable acceptance” or “acceptance-oriented change control”; reserve Nuclear-grade Context Engineering for the reference implementation/project name.
12. **Strengthen related work.** Add and differentiate verified adjacent work, especially *Software Delegation Contracts*. Also address governed oversight, governed context, runtime governance, and execution provenance where verified.
13. **Rewrite the conclusion.** Lead with the evidence self-authorship problem and the falsifiable prediction, not the lifecycle.

## Rejected or qualified suggestions

- **Do not claim evidence self-authorship is the dominant failure mode.** The current evidence does not establish prevalence or dominance. Call it a distinct and under-controlled failure mode.
- **Do not preserve “five graded rungs” merely because one review called them novel.** A second review correctly identifies the partial-order defect; conceptual soundness outranks branding value.
- **Do not add external-study results that do not exist.** v0.2 remains a discussion preprint. The proposed independent study belongs in the research agenda.
- **Do not cite review prose as evidence.** Every new adjacent-work claim must be verified against a primary source.
- **Do not expand the manuscript with more templates or doctrine.** The academic revision is a subtraction-and-clarification pass.

## Intended claim after revision

AI-assisted changes create a distinct acceptance risk when the same coupled process authors both a candidate change and the evidence consumed by its gates. A consequence-graded acceptance record and explicit evidence-independence profile can make that coupling inspectable and testable using ordinary repository workflows. The public implementation demonstrates feasibility; efficacy remains an open empirical question.

## Publication posture

- Defensible now: arXiv discussion preprint, position paper, or industry/practice venue submission.
- Not yet defensible: claims of improved correctness, reviewer accuracy, defect reduction, safety, compliance, or organizational outcomes.
- Strongest next empirical step: preregistered, blinded comparison with seeded plausible defects and multiple independent reviewers.
