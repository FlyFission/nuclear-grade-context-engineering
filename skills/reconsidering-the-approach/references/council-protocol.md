# Optional council deliberation protocol

Load on Standard, Deep or Creative sessions only; the main skill remains small.

| Depth | Seats | Method |
|---|---|---|
| Quick | Three opposing lenses | One alternative, challenge, action |
| Standard | Five functional lenses | Sealed first positions when feasible, anonymized critique, preserved dissent |
| Deep | Up to seven genuinely differentiated lenses | Cross-exam, disagreement map, meta-audit and actual panel-health disclosure |
| Creative | Five to seven | Eight distinct mechanisms before critique, then convergence |

Default five lenses are First Principles, User/Product, Systems/Operations, Contrarian/Inversion and Craft/Execution. Custom historical thinkers are method analogies. Select two or three fitting mental models, not every one by default.

Distinguish viewpoint diversity from independent model/technical review. A single AI with five personas is one model family. A high-consequence independent verdict belongs to `running-a-review-panel`, with frozen evidence, declared independence axes, verified findings and recorded degradation.

Anonymized challenge must identify the strongest insight, strongest flaw, and one falsifiable improvement. The synthesis records common ground, central divergence, a minority singleton, blind spots, and the test that would reverse the conclusion. Reject options violating non-negotiable constraints. Scoring needs justified weights and sensitivity; experiments require equal acceptance criteria and existing execution authority. Do not add duplicate approval machinery.
