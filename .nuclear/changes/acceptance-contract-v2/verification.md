### Propagation measurement

Run before the rule was used. Of 56 Standard-style records, 35 were clean, 14 tainted, and 7 had no claim row the parser could read. Used as a `validate_packet` failure it would have failed 21 of 56 for format as much as for evidence, and it treats evidence-id rows such as `E-005` as claims. This is why it is advisory.
