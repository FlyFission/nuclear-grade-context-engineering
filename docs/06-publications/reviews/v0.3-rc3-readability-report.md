# WATE v0.3 RC3 Readability Report

Date: 2026-07-20

## Scope

This report compares the merged v0.3 RC2 manuscript at `origin/main` with the RC3 presentation revision. RC3 changes readability and figures only. It does not add Council material, research claims, citations, or efficacy claims.

## Method

The measurement used `textstat` for Flesch-Kincaid grade, Flesch Reading Ease, Gunning Fog, and SMOG. A deterministic extraction step removed LaTeX comments, citations, labels, math, bibliography commands, figures, tables, TikZ, and display-only blocks. `pylatexenc` then converted the remaining LaTeX narrative to plain text.

These formulas are approximate. Technical names and formal terms raise the scores even when the prose is clear. The results should be read as comparison signals, not as proof that every reader will understand the paper.

## Punctuation check

Publication-facing RC3 source was checked for:

- Unicode em dash, U+2014;
- LaTeX triple hyphen, `---`.

Result: zero occurrences in `paper.tex` and `arxiv-metadata.txt`.

The title retains a typographic en dash between Actor and Evidence. An en dash is not an em dash.

## Interpretation

RC3 does not force every technical phrase to grade 12. It instead uses grade 12 as the default narrative layer and keeps higher scores where the paper must name formal methods, prior art, provenance concepts, or statistical study design. The abstract, problem formulation, implementation overview, five-axis model, worked example, adoption section, and conclusion now provide accessible entry points for mixed audiences.
